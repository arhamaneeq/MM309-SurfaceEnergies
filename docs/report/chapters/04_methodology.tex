\section{Computational methodology}
Bulk and slab structures were generated with the Atomic Simulation Environment and simulated in LAMMPS using the project MEAM files \texttt{library.meam} and \texttt{FeNiAl.meam}. Three seeds (20261024--20261026) generated the chemical configurations. Each bulk configuration contains a nominal $4\times4\times4$ conventional FCC supercell (256 sites before any implementation-dependent changes).

Bulk systems were energy-minimised and equilibrated at target temperature and zero pressure using NPT, with a 1~fs timestep, 5~ps equilibration, and 10~ps production. The mean potential energy per atom supplies the bulk reference; the mean volume gives an equivalent FCC lattice parameter $a=(4V/N)^{1/3}$.

Slabs were constructed from temperature-specific bulk structures with 12 atomic layers and 10~\AA{} vacuum per side. Periodicity was retained in-plane and removed along the surface normal. After positional minimisation at fixed cell dimensions, slabs were sampled under NVT for 5~ps equilibration and 10~ps production. The calculation matrix comprises $3$ compositions $\times3$ temperatures $\times3$ configurations $\times3$ orientations $=81$ slab runs, alongside 27 bulk runs.

For a slab with two equivalent faces, the estimator is
\begin{equation}
\gamma(t)=\frac{E_{\mathrm{slab}}(t)-N e_{\mathrm{bulk}}(T)}{2A},
\label{eq:gamma}
\end{equation}
where $A$ is the area of one face and $e_{\mathrm{bulk}}$ is the matching bulk reference energy per atom. Values in eV/\AA$^2$ were converted using $1$~eV/\AA$^2=16.0218$~J/m$^2$. This is an excess-potential-energy estimate, not a complete finite-temperature surface free energy. Three configuration-level trajectory means are used to calculate the reported mean and sample standard deviation.

The structure-generation scripts, LAMMPS input files, post-processing
routines, and plotting scripts are maintained in the project repository
\cite{project_repository}. Simulation settings and processed results
are retained alongside the analysis code to support reproducibility.
