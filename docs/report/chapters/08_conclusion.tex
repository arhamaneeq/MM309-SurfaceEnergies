\section{Conclusions}
For the sampled FCC-based Fe--Ni--Al models, (111) has the lowest surface-energy estimate and (110) the highest. Fe$_{20}$Ni$_{50}$Al$_{30}$ has the highest estimates overall and Fe$_{50}$Ni$_{30}$Al$_{20}$ the lowest. Temperature effects are comparatively weak and not uniformly monotonic, while configuration-to-configuration variability is substantial for several conditions. Bulk lattice parameters increase with temperature.

These are preliminary potential-energy-based results, not validated thermodynamic surface free energies. The priority is to resolve the atom-type mapping discrepancy, verify the bulk reference convention, and repeat geometry convergence tests for production slabs. Longer trajectories and additional configurations are needed before small temperature differences can be interpreted confidently.
