\section{Preliminary convergence tests}
Thickness and vacuum tests used a separate Fe$_{25}$Ni$_{25}$Al$_{50}$ FCC (111) test slab, not one of the production compositions. The thickness series was non-monotonic for thin slabs; estimates for 12 and 14 layers were 1.4324 and 1.4376~J/m$^2$, respectively, a difference of 0.0052~J/m$^2$ (about 0.36\% of the 14-layer value). For a 10-layer test slab, 2~\AA{} vacuum per side changed the energy by 0.75037~eV relative to the 25~\AA{} reference, while 4~\AA{} and larger padding matched the reference at the reported precision.

These tests motivate 12 layers and 10~\AA{} vacuum as provisional settings, not universal convergence criteria. Critically, the atom-type mapping used in the convergence dataset has not yet been reconciled with the production mapping. The tests must be repeated after that audit, and convergence should be checked for the production composition and (110) geometry before treating the settings as validated.
